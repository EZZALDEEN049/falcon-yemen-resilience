Article title: Structural-Functional Divergence During Submarine-Cable Disruptions: Evidence from Yemen's FALCON Dependence
Authors: Ezzaldeen Al-Tayar; Abdullateef Ali
Affiliation: Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology, Taiz University, Taiz, Yemen
Corresponding author: Abdullateef Ali

Description:
Clean reproducibility package containing supporting derived data, author-generated figures, analysis code, robustness outputs, validation logs, textual provenance, and primary-data acquisition/reconstruction scripts.

Third-party IODA source plot images are intentionally NOT redistributed in this package. The package retains the digitized-signal table, complete sensitivity-result table, method specification, and provenance note required to audit the reported derived result.
