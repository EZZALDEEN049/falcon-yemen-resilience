# Provenance note

The central IODA comparison in the manuscript was derived from published normalized IODA plots for AS30873 and AS12486 covering 7–10 January 2020.

The original third-party plot images are **not redistributed in this supplementary package**. They were removed from the submission package to avoid republication of third-party artwork without an explicit redistribution permission. The package retains:

- the digitized signal table used for the reported central comparison;
- the complete sensitivity-result table generated during the authors' audit;
- the digitization-method specification;
- scripts that document the original extraction method; and
- source/provenance descriptions pointing to IODA as the originating measurement platform.

Researchers who wish to rerun pixel-level extraction from the original plots must obtain the corresponding IODA plots independently from the IODA source/archive and place them locally using the filenames documented in `data/stage9_ioda_digitization_method_v0_1.json`.

No third-party source image is included in Online Resource 1.
